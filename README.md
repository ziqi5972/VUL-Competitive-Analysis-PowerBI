# VUL Competitive Analysis | Power BI

## Overview

Developed a Power BI competitive analysis of Variable Universal Life (VUL) products across four major U.S. insurers: **Equitable, Lincoln Financial, Nationwide, and Pacific Life**.

The project transforms complex insurance product information into a standardized analytical framework covering **fees, historical performance, guarantees & riders, investment architecture, and liquidity provisions**.

## Project Scope

- **4 U.S. insurers**
- **4 representative VUL products**
- **20+ fee and charge categories**
- **15+ guarantee and rider categories**
- **1-year, 3-year, and 5-year performance metrics**
- Investment architecture, policy loan, withdrawal, and surrender analysis

## Dashboard 1 — Performance & Fees

Analyzes historical investment performance and product-level cost structures, including surrender charges, administrative fees, premium charges, loan interest, and fund expenses.

![Performance and Fee Dashboard](Performance%20and%20Fee.png)

## Dashboard 2 — Guarantees & Riders

Compares product differentiation through no-lapse guarantees, long-term care benefits, chronic illness benefits, accelerated death benefits, loan protection provisions, and death benefit structures.

The analysis identified **5 features for Equitable, 4 each for Nationwide and Pacific Life, and 3 for Lincoln Financial** within the selected product materials.

![Guarantees and Riders Dashboard](Guarantees%20and%20Riders.png)

## Dashboard 3 — Investment Architecture & Liquidity

Compares variable investment options, fixed/guaranteed accounts, indexed strategies, policy loans, withdrawals, surrender provisions, associated charges, and key restrictions.

![Investment and Liquidity Dashboard](Investment%20and%20Liquidity.png)

## Key Insights

- VUL competitiveness extends beyond investment performance to **fees, guarantees, riders, investment flexibility, and liquidity**.
- All four analyzed products provide combinations of **variable, fixed/guaranteed, and indexed investment options**, but their underlying structures differ.
- Guarantee and rider availability varies materially across insurers.
- Loan and withdrawal provisions can affect **cash value, death benefits, and policy sustainability**.
- Fee structures use different definitions and units, requiring component-level comparison rather than simple aggregation.

## Methodology

1. Collected product information from publicly available insurer materials.
2. Extracted and cleaned product-level data.
3. Standardized inconsistent terminology across insurers.
4. Structured fees, performance, riders, investment, and liquidity data into comparable analytical categories.
5. Built the data model and interactive dashboards in Power BI.
